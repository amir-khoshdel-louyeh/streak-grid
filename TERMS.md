# Terms of Service — StreakGrid (Phase 6, shared)

Effective: 2026-10-15 (v1). Contact: [SUPPORT-EMAIL — fill before public release].

## 1. The deal

StreakGrid is an offline-first habit tracker. A free account requires a
one-time online activation; afterwards the app works fully offline with a
30-day grace between checks (`core/docs/LICENSING.md §4`).

## 2. Free vs Pro

- Free: up to 5 active habits, 2 devices, core tracking forever.
- Pro (coming soon, via Stripe/Lemon Squeezy): unlimited habits/devices,
  export, widgets. Pro is an account entitlement, enforced from the cached
  plan — it never needs the network at point of use.

## 3. Acceptable use & enforcement

One account per person; no credential sharing, no reverse-engineering the
registry, no abuse of other users. Violations may lead to `suspended` or
`banned` status. Enforcement is account-level and takes effect on the next
online check: the app becomes read-only with the reason shown. **Your local
data is never deleted by a lock** — export it, appeal, or delete the account.

## 4. Payments & refunds (pro, when launched)

Handled by the payment provider's terms; entitlements update automatically
via webhook. Refund window: 14 days, no questions asked.

## 5. Termination & deletion

You may stop using StreakGrid anytime; uninstalling removes local data per
platform. Registry deletion on request (`server/RUNBOOK.md`), confirmed
within 30 days. We may terminate accounts for §3 violations with prior
notice except for active abuse.

Provided "as is", without warranty. We are not liable for missed habits.
